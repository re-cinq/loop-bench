## Divergence from the issue, decided on this branch

The issue's criteria ask for an opt-in `transport.history` flag (off by default, an `error` frame when off), an `UNSUPPORTED` error for a store with no `listFor`, and an exported `StoreCannotList`; the acceptance tests this branch records instead answer a bare `list_sessions` with `{type: 'session_list', sessions: []}` when the store has no `listFor`, with no flag.

Recorded decision: the tests' shape stands. The frames ship as `list_sessions`/`session_list`, answered from the connection's own authentication; the deployer's opt-in is the store they inject, since `listFor` is optional and a store that cannot list never exposes a history; a store without `listFor` answers an empty list, and a failing one answers `SERVER_ERROR` with its detail in the `ws` log. What the decision costs, stated so it is not silent: both shipped stores implement `listFor`, so a default deployment makes a user's conversation metadata reachable with no act by the deployer — precisely the second disclosure the issue wanted a flag to keep auditable. Reopening it (the flag, `UNSUPPORTED`, `StoreCannotList`) rewrites the three recorded acceptance tests and is the ticket owner's call, not a repair round's; it stays an open facet below rather than being dropped.

## Out of scope

- Conversation titles or previews (first user message, model-generated title).
- Deleting or renaming a conversation from the client over the socket.
- Switching the socket's active session in place (`?sessionId=` on reconnect is how a conversation is rejoined).
- Holding pagination cursor state server-side (the client pages with `before`).
- An operator view of another user's conversations or live sync between open tabs.
